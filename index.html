Sky Peek

Look Up. Stay Curious.

By ZDI

Sky Peek shows what is above you: aircraft around you now, and in future updates the satellites and Starlink trains that may be visible overhead.

The distance rings measure ground distance from your position only. They are not a guide to what you can see in the sky.

Your location stays in your browser. It is used to centre the map and fetch nearby aircraft, and is not sent to a Sky Peek server. If you deny location access, the map shows a default city instead.

Aircraft data: adsb.fi open data, for personal, non-commercial use. Map: OpenStreetMap contributors.
