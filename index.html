Sky Peek

Look Up. Stay Curious.

By ZDI

Sky Peek shows what is above you: aircraft around your location now, with satellite and Starlink passes to follow in a later update.

The rings mark ground distance from your position, 10 km apart. They are a sense of scale, not a guide to what is visible in the sky.

Your location is kept in your browser and used to centre the map. To find nearby aircraft, the selected coordinates are sent to the aircraft data provider with each request. Sky Peek has no server of its own and stores nothing.

Aircraft data: adsb.fi open data, for personal, non-commercial use. Map: OpenStreetMap contributors, dark basemap by Esri.
